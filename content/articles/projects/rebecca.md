<p>Age: KS3</p></br>

<p><strong>Overall aim:</strong></p>
<ul>
    <li>to consider students’ own responses to the climate crisis</li>
    <li>to explore the idea of collective responsibility</li>
    <li>thinking about connectedness and togetherness </li>
</ul>

<ol>Description: 
    <li>Pupils will consider the purpose and value of all species</li>
    <li>Pupils will discuss and debate the idea of what is most important, including the environment</li>
    <li>Pupils will reflect on biblical narratives</li>
    <li>Pupils will think about themselves as individuals, as well as members of local, national and global communities.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>By the end of the unit</strong>:</p>

<p>Pupils will create a piece of art at the beginning and the end of the unit to express their responses to the climate crisis. Over the course of the unit, they will be challenged to see themselves as individuals and members of local and global communities involved in the climate crisis, not onlookers but actors. They will reflect on their feelings at the end of the unit through art.</p>
