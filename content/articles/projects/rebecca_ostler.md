<p>Age: Key Stage 2-3</p></br>

<p><strong>Overall aim:</strong> To generate tricky questions, to work together to explore them, to consider if, why and how we should care for our planet</p>

<p><strong>Description:</strong></p>
<p>Some of the tricky questions pupils will engage with:</p>
<ol>
    <li>Why are other species important?</li>
    <li>What can other species contribute to the ecological debate?</li>
    <li>How can we work to restore nature?</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Topics pupils engage with:</strong>:</p>

<ol>
    <li>The Sycamore Gap tree. Exploring how important this act is, and what action could be taken.</li>
    <li>Learning about elephants as ecological gardeners, and how animals, such as chimpanzees, work with humans.</li>
    <li>Exploring the question of what we share with other species and how we differ, drawing on the insights of Jane Goodall</li>
    <li>Considering the guidance the Bible provides regarding the natural world, looking at different interpretations of key texts</li>
    <li>Asking what can we do to restore nature and the idea of an ecologically focussed Shabbat/Sabbath</li>
</ol>
