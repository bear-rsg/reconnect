<p><strong>Overall aim:</strong></p>
<p>In this set of four lessons for KS3, students have the opportunity to explore different perspectives, including non-human perspectives, to make sense of climate breakdown through stories.</p>

<p><strong>Description:</strong></p>
<ul>
    <li>Students will hear from young people around the world, their fears, hopes and creative plans.</li>
    <li>Students will consider ancient wisdom in stories of creation and how they teach us to cherish planet Earth.</li>
    <li>Students will find out about what inspires groups and individuals to take action, and what this action looks like.</li>
    <li>Students will reflect on their own experiences and worldviews, and consider how their understanding has grown.</li>
</ul>

<p>Stories:
<br />Earth’s Story – <strong>What is the earth’s climate story?</strong>
<br />Stories of Origin - <strong>The value of earth in creation stories</strong>
<br />Stories of Inspiration - <strong>How can stories inspire climate action?</strong>
<br />My Climate Story – <strong>What is my own climate story?</strong>
</p>
