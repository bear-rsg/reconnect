Team / Alumni

Current Team

Kate Christopher

Kate Christopher is a secondary teacher and independent RE adviser. Kate's PhD focussed on the Philosophy of Education and has a passion for curriculum design, finding ways to offer a more textured and critical understanding of the world for 21st Century pupils.

Ian Jones

Ian Jones is the Director of St Peter’s Saltley Trust, and the co-director of the RE:Connect project. He has a PhD in church history, with additional experience as a social researcher specialising in Christianity and social change in modern/contemporary Britain.

Jeremy Kidwell

Jeremy Kidwell is an associate professor in theological ethics at the University of Birmingham and co-director of the RE:Connect project.
